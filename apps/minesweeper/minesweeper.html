How to play

  • Left-click to reveal a cell.
  • Right-click to flag/unflag a suspected mine.
  • A revealed number tells you how many mines are adjacent.
  • Chord click (left+right on a number with the right number of flags around it) auto-reveals the rest.
  • The first click is always safe — mines are placed afterwards.
  • Reveal every non-mine cell to win!
